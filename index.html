REAL ESTATE GUIDANCE

Protect your property investment.

Important considerations every property buyer and investor should understand before completing a real estate transaction.

Before purchasing a property, make sure the ownership documents and title information are properly reviewed and verified.

Inspect the property and consider its location, accessibility, surrounding environment and other factors before making a decision.

Clearly document the agreed property details, responsibilities, costs, payment terms and other important transaction information.

Review the condition of the property and understand any repairs, maintenance or improvements that may be required.

Agree on clear payment terms and make sure all transaction costs and financial obligations are properly understood before proceeding.

Before completing the transaction, ensure the required documents, keys, access and ownership arrangements are properly handed over.
