CONSTRUCTION GUIDANCE

Build with confidence.

Important considerations every client should understand before starting a construction project.

Before construction begins, the site, project requirements, design and other important conditions should be properly assessed and planned.

Ensure the appropriate architectural, structural and technical plans are prepared and that required approvals are properly handled before construction begins.

Construction materials and workmanship should meet the requirements of the project and be properly monitored throughout the construction process.

Understand the estimated project cost, materials, labour, professional fees and other applicable expenses before work begins.

Proper supervision helps ensure that work follows the approved plans, quality requirements and appropriate site safety practices.

Before project completion, final inspections, corrections and necessary documentation should be handled before the building is officially handed over.
