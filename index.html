BUILD WITH CONFIDENCE

Protect your investment.

Important considerations every property owner should understand before and during construction.

Before beginning a project, confirm that the property ownership and title documentation are properly reviewed and verified.

Make sure the appropriate planning approvals, permits and statutory requirements are addressed before construction begins.

Clearly document project scope, responsibilities, costs, materials, timelines and other important project terms.

Structural work should be properly designed, reviewed and executed by qualified professionals appropriate to the project.

Agree on clear project milestones and payment stages so progress and payments can be properly tracked throughout the project.

Before final handover, inspect the completed work and document any outstanding items that need to be addressed.
